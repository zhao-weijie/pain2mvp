# Discovery Rubric

Use this rubric to score each clustered opportunity from 0 to 100.

## 1. Recurrence (0-25)

Measure how often the same underlying pain appears across independent users, threads, and time.

- **0-5**: one-off mention or one noisy thread
- **6-12**: repeated mentions, but limited breadth
- **13-19**: recurring across multiple users or communities
- **20-25**: frequent, repeated, and independently corroborated

## 2. Severity and urgency (0-20)

Measure how painful the issue sounds in the user's workflow.

Signals:
- blocked work
- lost time
- compliance or quality risk
- revenue, deadline, or client impact
- emotionally strong but specific language tied to consequences

## 3. Workaround burden (0-20)

Measure how much manual effort or tool stitching users describe.

Signals:
- copy-paste chains
- spreadsheets, docs, or ad hoc scripts
- repeated review loops
- switching between multiple products
- hiring or outsourcing because the workflow is too brittle

## 4. Buying or switching signal (0-15)

Measure whether users hint at willingness to pay, switch, or actively search for alternatives.

Signals:
- "I would pay for..."
- "I switched from X to Y because..."
- "Looking for a tool that..."
- explicit dissatisfaction with current tooling paired with intent to replace it

## 5. Evidence quality and freshness (0-20)

Measure whether the evidence is strong enough to trust.

High score when:
- multiple independent users say similar things
- at least one concrete workflow example is present
- evidence is recent
- both Reddit and Hacker News support the cluster, or one source has especially strong repeated evidence

Lower the score when:
- evidence is old
- evidence is dominated by hype, memes, or generic discussion
- the cluster depends on one charismatic post
- the issue looks like a niche setup problem rather than a category-level pain

## Confidence rating

Report confidence separately from the score.

- **High**: repeated, recent, concrete, and independently corroborated
- **Medium**: enough signal to prioritize, but still patchy or source-concentrated
- **Low**: interesting hypothesis, but evidence is thin or weak

## Query heuristics

### Idea-led research

Expand the idea into:
- the workflow it replaces
- the job the user is trying to complete
- adjacent roles who may feel the same pain
- failure modes or manual steps likely to exist today

Search for language such as:
- frustrating
- manual
- time-consuming
- error-prone
- bottleneck
- workaround
- spreadsheet
- copy paste
- review loop
- impossible to scale

### User-group research

Search for:
- role title variants
- workflow nouns
- known tools used by that role
- complaints about process, review, coordination, drafting, or compliance

### Competitor-led research

Separate complaints into:
- missing capability
- output quality
- speed and reliability
- onboarding and usability
- pricing and value
- trust, security, or policy
- support and change management

Do not collapse all complaints into one cluster just because the vendor name is the same.
